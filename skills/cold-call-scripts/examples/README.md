# Cold-call scripts — worked example

`before.txt` is the pitch-at-you opener: fake pleasantry, jargon, a demo ask, a 30-minute ask, and no reason for the call. `after.txt` follows the skill: it says it's a cold call, asks permission for 30 seconds, leads with a signal (new role plus SDR hiring), names the problem it usually creates, gives one line on help, and ends with a question. `voicemail.txt` is under 20 seconds and names the subject line of the paired email.

Names, company and the routing claim are illustrative. No statistics are used. Pinned by `tests/call-examples.test.mjs`.
