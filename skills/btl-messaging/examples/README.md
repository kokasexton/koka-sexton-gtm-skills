# BTL messaging — worked example

`before.txt` pitches ROI to a practitioner, asks for a call and threatens to loop in their VP. `after.txt` follows the skill: an observation about their work in their words, something useful now, a low-commitment ask, and an offer to arm them as an internal champion. Under ~120 words.

The name and post are illustrative; no statistic is claimed. Lint it: `node skills/cold-email/scripts/lint-email.mjs skills/btl-messaging/examples/after.txt`.
