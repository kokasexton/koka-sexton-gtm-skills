# DM scripts: Alex Rivera

Two scenarios, chosen because they are what Alex will meet most in the next month: someone relevant **connects**, and someone **comments** on a post.

## Scenario 1: a founder connects
**Trigger:** a seed or Series A founder accepts a request or sends one, and Alex has seen their profile. Alex knows their company and stage, nothing more.

**Opener A:** Thanks for connecting, `[[FILL: first name]]`. I saw `[[FILL: one specific thing on their profile, e.g. the product or the stage]]`. Are you planning a raise this year, or heads-down on building?

**Opener B:** Good to be connected, `[[FILL: first name]]`. What's the finance question that eats most of your time right now?

**The ask:** one question they can answer in a line. No call, no link, no pitch.

**Reply paths**
- **Yes (raising or stuck):** ask one follow-up about their timeline, then offer a short call. Book it plainly, no more selling.
- **Not now:** "No problem. I'll leave you to it. If a raise comes up, message me." Note a date to check back.
- **No or not relevant:** thank them and stop.

**Silence:** one follow-up after a week or so, with something new, like a post that matches their stage. Then stop.

## Scenario 2: someone comments on a post
**Trigger:** a founder or investor leaves a real comment on one of Alex's posts, more than a reaction.

**Opener A:** Thanks for the comment on the data room post, `[[FILL: first name]]`. Your point about `[[FILL: what they actually said]]` is the part founders get wrong most. Is that something you're dealing with right now?

**Opener B:** Appreciate you adding that, `[[FILL: first name]]`. Did that come from something you've been through?

**The ask:** one question about their experience.

**Reply paths:** the same three as above. If they share a real problem, offer to look at it for twenty minutes, no charge for the first look `[[FILL: only if Alex really offers this]]`.

## Do not
- Paste a pitch or attach a deck in the first message.
- Message anyone who has said no.
- Send the same text to a list. Every message carries a real detail about the person.
- Automate any of this. Alex sends each message by hand.
