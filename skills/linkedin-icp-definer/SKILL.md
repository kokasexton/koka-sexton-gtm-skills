# LinkedIn ICP Definer

Produce one locked ICP definition that every other skill scores against. A vague ICP makes every downstream skill guess.

## The five sections

1. **Offer** — what's sold, the price band, the outcome promised.
2. **Firmographics** — industries, company size, geography, tech/stack signals.
3. **Persona** — titles, seniority, function, the pain they own.
4. **Triggers** — events that create urgency (new exec, funding, hiring, tech change).
5. **Disqualifiers** — explicit "no" (IC-level, wrong function, procurement-heavy enterprise, agencies with no budget, etc.).

## Method

Come with a draft, not a blank page: propose defaults for each section, then ask the user to confirm, reject, or refine — two or three questions at a time. Precision beats coverage: a sharp ICP of 200 accounts beats a fuzzy one of 20,000.

## Output

A single ICP document with the five sections plus a scoring rubric (how to grade a prospect A–F). Every other skill reads this and nothing else.

## Routing
- Build it from evidence (won/lost deals, 100-point fit score) → `define-icp`
- Tier accounts against it → `qualify-accounts`
- Who to warm first → `linkedin-warm-list-builder`
- Score a post's engagers → `linkedin-engager-analytics`
