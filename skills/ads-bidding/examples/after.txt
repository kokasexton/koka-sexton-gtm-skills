Bidding plan (new ABM program):
1. New campaign, no history: start on automated maximum delivery to get a cost baseline. Move to a cost cap only once you know your acceptable cost per result.
2. Small tier-1 audience that must be reached in full: use a manual bid, because you control competitiveness for a narrow, high-value audience.
3. Budget by layer: most early spend goes to layer 1, which fills the retargeting pools. Retargeting budgets stay small because the pools are small. Tier-1 accounts get disproportionate spend per account.
4. Fewer, better campaigns: do not spread thin, because campaigns without enough delivery never learn.
When costs spike: check audience size and frequency, then creative fatigue, then competition such as end of quarter. Only then change the bid, in one step, and wait before judging.
Confirm bid options and minimums in Campaign Manager. Use your own account history for cost targets.
