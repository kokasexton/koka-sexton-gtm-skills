Conversions API connected through the CRM so meeting held and opportunity created flow back. Report order: outcome first (pipeline created or influenced at target accounts), then account (target accounts engaged, and how many took a meeting), then audience (seniority and function mix), then efficiency (cost per engaged target account, cost per qualified meeting). CTR, CPM and frequency go last as diagnostics only.
