# LinkedIn Signal Stack

For a target, assemble at least five specific, sourced signals — the raw material that makes a message feel written by someone paying attention. A message that references one real thing they said beats ten templated lines.

## Signal classes (aim for 5+ across these)

1. **Their own words** — a post or comment you actually read. Quote the idea, not the sentence.
2. **Engagement** — what they engage with, whose content, how deeply.
3. **Role & timing** — a new role, promotion, or fresh mandate (buyers in their first ~90 days build stacks).
4. **Company** — funding, launch, hiring surge, tech change, press.
5. **Shared thread** — a person, project, or community you both sit in.

## Rules

- Every signal needs a source (link + date). No source, no signal.
- Signals that map to the ICP's pains and triggers outrank trivia.
- Never imply you read something you didn't.

## Output

A signal stack per target: 5+ sourced signals, plus two or three angles they unlock for outreach.

## Routing
- Write with the stack → `personalization`, `cold-email`
- Warm them first → `linkedin-comment-warmer`
