# Personalization Sources

Rank sources by how much they say about **this person's priorities right now**.

| Rank | Bucket | Examples | Where | Fresh for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Their own words** | A post, a comment, a podcast answer, a talk | LinkedIn activity, podcasts, events | Weeks |
| 2 | **Their engagement with you** | Commented on your post, attended your session | Your engagers, `skills/warm-list` | Days to weeks |
| 3 | **A change** | New role, team growth, funding, launch, tech switch | `skills/signal-sourcer` | Weeks to a quarter |
| 4 | **Their team's public work** | A job description they wrote, a case study, a launch post | Careers page, company blog | Weeks |
| 5 | **Shared context** | Same event, mutual connection, same former employer | Your network | Only if real and relevant |
| 6 | **Company story** | Positioning, market, recent news | Website, press | Months: the weakest bucket |

## How to research in 5 minutes

1. **Their recent activity** (posts and comments, last 30 days). Anything about the problem you solve?
2. **Your engagement data.** Have they interacted with you?
3. **Signals at the account.** Anything changed?
4. Pick **the single strongest** observation. One. Write it down in a sentence.
5. If you've found nothing in buckets 1–4, the person isn't ready for a personal note. Use a value-first segment message or wait.

## Never use

Personal life details, family, private social accounts, location tracking, or anything that would feel like surveillance if they knew how you found it.
