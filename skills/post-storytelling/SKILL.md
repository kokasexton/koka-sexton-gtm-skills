# Post Storytelling

Dwell time is a ranking signal. People keep reading a post that teaches one thing clearly, with room to breathe.

## What the data says

- **Long beats short:** 1,500+ char posts earn about 2× the median of short ones (74 vs ~40).
- **Whitespace:** line breaks lift engagement +72% (62 vs 36).
- **How-to and tactical posts:** median 69. AI/automation tops the topics (76). **Teach the shift, don't provoke.**

## Pick one lesson

Say it in one sentence before you write. If you can't, you don't have a post yet; you have a topic.

## Three structures

**Problem → Agitate → Solve.** For a mistake your audience makes.
> Name the problem → show what it costs (with a number) → the fix, as steps.

**Before → After → Bridge.** For a result.
> Where you or a client started → where it ended up → exactly what changed.

**Attention → Interest → Desire → Action.** For an idea or a framework.
> The surprising fact → why it's true → what it means for them → one thing to try.

## Craft rules

- One idea per line on mobile. Short paragraphs, generous breaks.
- **Specifics over adjectives.** "12 comments from Directors" beats "amazing engagement."
- Cite every number. Source it or cut it.
- **End with a real question** the ICP can answer from experience. That's how you get substantive comments (see `skills/post-cta`).
