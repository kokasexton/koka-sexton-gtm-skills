# Personalization

Real personalisation comes from an observation, not a variable. If you didn't observe it, it isn't personal — it's a placeholder.

## Sources of real personalisation

1. **Their own words** — a post or comment you actually read. Quote the idea, not the sentence.
2. **Public behaviour** — engagement, follows, the accounts they operate in.
3. **A change** — new role, funding, launch, hire, tech move.
4. **A shared thread** — a person, project, or conversation you both sit in.

## Rules

- One real observation beats three generic compliments.
- Never imply you read something you didn't.
- Specificity signals effort; effort signals intent.
- If you have no observation, don't fake it — don't send.

## Anti-patterns

"Love your content!", "Huge fan of your work", "Saw you're in [industry]" — these read as templates because they are. Cut them.
