# LinkedIn comment warmer: worked example

`post.txt` is the target's post. `before.txt` is the comment the skill blocks: empty affirmation, jargon, a pitch and a connection ask in a public thread. `after.txt` follows the skill: it adds one specific experience, stays on the author's topic, makes no ask, and ends on a question the author can answer in one line.

| Check from the skill | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| Adds something specific (a fact, counterpoint or experience) | No | Yes: contracts, a rolling 13 weeks, renewals as their own line |
| On the author's topic, not the commenter's product | No | Yes |
| No pitch, link, tag or connection ask | No | Yes |
| 2 to 4 sentences, 40+ characters | No (empty praise) | Yes (4 sentences) |
| Worth reading if the author never checked who wrote it | No | Yes |

Dana, Harbor & Pine and the commenter's client are **fictional**. Nothing here is a real person or result. No statistics are used. Pinned by `tests/comment-examples.test.mjs`.
