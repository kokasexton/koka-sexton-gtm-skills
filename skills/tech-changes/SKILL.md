# Tech Changes

A stack change is a moment when the company is already open to change, has budget allocated, and is rethinking workflows.

## Read the change

| Change | Signal | Play |
|---|---|---|
| **Adopted a complementary tool** (one you integrate with) | Your value just went up | "Now that you're on X, here's what teams add next…" |
| **Dropped a tool you replace** | Gap or active evaluation | Help them avoid the mistake that made them drop it |
| **Migrating platforms** (e.g. a CRM switch) | Every connected tool is up for review | Offer to be the easy part of the migration |
| **Adopted your competitor** | They bought the category; they're not lost | Nurture; watch for renewal or competitor signals |
| **Hiring for a tool skill** | A JD names a tool (see `skills/hiring-signals`) | Confirms the stack |

## Where to see it

Technographic lookup tools, job descriptions, integration marketplaces and partner directories, public case studies, and employee posts ("we just moved to X").

## Message

> Saw the team is moving to a new CRM this quarter. The migration is the moment to fix lead routing, before old habits get rebuilt. Here's the checklist we use.
