# Ads Creative

The feed rewards content that looks like content. Ads that look like ads get scrolled past.

## Formats, in rough order of fit for B2B

| Format | Use it for | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Thought Leader Ads** (a member's post, sponsored) | Layer 1 attention | A person's voice; reads as content. Promote posts that already earned organic comments. |
| **Document ads** | Teaching: frameworks, teardowns, benchmarks | Native, swipeable. In the organic data, documents earned a high comment share (21.7%). |
| **Single image** | One clear idea or number | Image formats beat link posts 2.8× organically, so build on that. |
| **Short video** | A face explaining one idea | Subtitled, front-loaded, under a minute. |
| **Conversation / message ads** | Layer 3 offers to engaged people | Only to warm audiences; cold, they feel like spam. |

## What to make

- **Promote your winners.** The best paid creative is usually an organic post that already earned substantive comments from the ICP. Sponsor it.
- **Teach one thing per ad.** A chart, a framework, a before/after.
- **Show a person.** Founder and practitioner faces beat stock photos and product screenshots.

## Fatigue and refresh

Watch frequency and engagement rate over time per creative. When engagement trends down while frequency climbs, the audience has seen it. Rotate in a new angle (not just a new colour) and keep 2–4 live creatives per campaign. Smaller ABM audiences fatigue faster, so plan a creative pipeline, not a launch.
