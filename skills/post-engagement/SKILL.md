# Post Engagement

Comments are the CRM.

## What the data says

- Commenters are **44.2% Director+ vs 35.1% of likers** (**26% more senior**).
- **78% of comments are substantive** (40+ chars). People who comment are telling you something.
- **91.9% of engagers engage with only one creator;** a hardcore few show up repeatedly. That repeating list is the warmest audience in B2B.

## On your posts

- **Reply to every comment**, with substance: answer, add a detail, ask a follow-up. Never just "Thanks!"
- **Reply to the reply.** Threads extend dwell time and signal conversation.
- **Note who's in the room.** ICP commenters go on the list (see `skills/warm-list`).

## On other people's posts (15 minutes a day)

- Comment on posts from **ICP buyers and the creators they follow**, adding a real point.
- Leave 2–3 thoughtful comments, not 30 shallow ones.
- That builds familiarity before any outreach (see `skills/linkedin-outreach-campaign`; `skills/linkedin-comment-warmer` drafts the comment).

## From comment to DM

Move to a DM only when there's a reason:
- They asked a question that needs more than a comment.
- They've engaged 2–3 times (a regular).
- You have something specific to send them.

> Thanks for the point on staffing. You asked how teams your size handle it, so I've put together the breakdown. Want it?

Never pitch in the first DM.

- Score who engaged → `skills/warm-list`
