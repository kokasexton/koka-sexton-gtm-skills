# Website Visitors

A pricing-page visit from a target account is the closest thing to raising a hand without raising a hand. A single blog visit is not.

## Which pages carry intent

| High | Medium | Low |
|---|---|---|
| Pricing, comparisons ("vs", "alternatives"), integrations, security, demo or contact pages | Case studies, product pages, docs | Blog, careers, home page only |

**Depth and recency matter:** several sessions or several people from one account in a short period beats one visit.

## Identification levels

- **Company level** (reverse-IP style): useful for account prioritisation and ads.
- **Person level** (where lawful and consented, via specialised visitor-ID tools): useful, but handle with extra care.
- **Known contacts** (clicked an email, logged in, filled a form): already in your CRM; route directly.

1. **Account visits high-intent pages** → add it to `skills/multi-signal` as a strong signal.
