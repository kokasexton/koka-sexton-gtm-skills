# Content Engagement

Of all the signals, this one shows you the person **thinking about the problem in public**.

## Depth beats volume

| Engagement | Strength | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Substantive comment** on your post (40+ chars) | Strongest | Commenters are 26% more senior than likers, and 78% of comments are substantive |
| **Repeat engagement** across 3+ posts | Strongest | A regular; the warmest audience in B2B |
| **Comment on a topic post** (someone else's post about your problem) | Strong | Public interest in the problem |
| **Webinar or event attendance**, document fully read | Strong | Time invested |
| **Repost** | Medium | Endorsement, sometimes passive |
| **Like** | Weak alone | Count it only with fit plus another signal |

## Sources

- **Your posts:** engagers export (run `skills/warm-list`)
- **Topic posts:** people commenting on posts about the problem you solve, from creators your ICP follows
- **Your events:** attendees, questions asked, replay viewers
- **Your ads:** see `skills/ads-outbound-sync`
