# Competitor Signals

The buyer comparing vendors is the most in-market buyer there is. Treat them like an adult with a decision to make.

## Signals

| Signal | Where | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **Category or comparison research** | Review-site intent (where you have it), your "vs" and alternatives pages, prompt-panel gaps (see `skills/prompt-panel`) | Strong |
| **Public dissatisfaction** | Negative reviews, community threads, social posts about a competitor | Strong, handle with tact |
| **Competitor customer near renewal** | Case studies, logos, tech lookups + contract-timing clues | Medium; time it |
| **Engaging with competitor content** | Comments on competitor posts and webinars | Medium |

## Plays

- **Comparison stage:** send an honest comparison: where you're better, where they are, and who each is for. Honesty wins trust and shortlists.
- **Dissatisfaction:** address the **problem**, not the competitor: "Teams moving off a tool that couldn't separate buyers from peers usually need…"
- **Renewal:** offer a migration plan and an easy evaluation **before** the renewal date, not after.

## Never

- Badmouth a competitor. Buyers read it as insecurity.
- Contact reviewers in ways the platform's terms don't allow.
- Mention that you saw their complaint. Talk about the problem.
