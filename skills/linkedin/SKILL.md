# LinkedIn

The highest-converting LinkedIn outreach almost never looks like outreach. It's a comment, then a reply, then a DM that continues a real exchange.

## The motion

```
Profile that converts ─▶ post & comment (linkedin-content) ─▶ they engage
        ▲                                                         │
        └─ safe pace (linkedin-safety) ◀─ DM on the exchange ◀── connect with a reason
                                          (linkedin-outreach-campaign)
```

| Job | Sub-skill |
|---|---|
| Make the profile do the converting | `skills/linkedin-profile` |
| Connection requests and DM sequences | `skills/linkedin-outreach-campaign` |
| A value-add comment that warms one target | `skills/linkedin-comment-warmer` |
| Limits, warm-up, and staying safe | `skills/linkedin-safety` |

## The data that shapes it

- Commenters are **26% more senior** than likers, so the comment section is where buyers show themselves (`skills/linkedin-algorithm`).
- **91.9% of engagers engage with only one creator.** The few who repeat are your warmest list.

## Rules

- **Engage before you ask.** A genuine comment on their post comes before the connection request.
- **Every connection request has a reason.** Every DM continues an exchange.
- **No pitch in the first DM.**
- **No automation** that acts as you. It's against LinkedIn's terms, and it's obvious.
