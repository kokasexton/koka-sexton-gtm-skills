# LinkedIn Ads

The same principle runs through the whole repo: **signal over vanity**. LinkedIn Ads are expensive per impression, so they only pay off when the audience is narrow, the creative earns attention, and the engagement turns into a conversation sales can have.

## The 3-layer funnel

| Layer | Audience | Goal | Creative | Sub-skill |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Attention** | ICP accounts × buying-committee titles | Earn the right to be remembered | Thought Leader Ads, document teaching posts, short video | `skills/ads-creative`, `skills/ads-copy` |
| **2. Consideration** | People who engaged with layer 1 | Deepen: proof, POV, comparisons | Case-study documents, webinar clips, conversation ads | `skills/ads-audiences` |
| **3. Conversion** | Engaged accounts and site visitors | One clear next step | Offer ads: audit, teardown, benchmark, demo | `skills/ads-campaign-setup` |

## The loop

1. **Audience.** Build it from the ICP and account list → `skills/ads-audiences`
2. **Structure.** Campaign groups, objectives, retargeting segments → `skills/ads-campaign-setup`
3. **Creative and copy.** Teach, don't advertise → `skills/ads-creative`, `skills/ads-copy`
4. **Budget and bids.** → `skills/ads-bidding`
5. **Measure** quality, not clicks → `skills/ads-measurement`
6. **Hand off.** Engaged accounts become sales signals → `skills/ads-outbound-sync`
7. **Fix** one lever at a time → `skills/ads-optimization`

## Principles

- **Narrow beats broad.** A small, exact audience of buyers is worth more than a large, cheap one.
- **Teach in the feed.** Native document and thought-leader formats echo the organic data: image and document formats beat link posts (see `skills/linkedin-algorithm`).
- **Ads warm, people close.** An ad impression isn't a lead. An engaged account plus a human follow-up is.
- **One change at a time**, so you know what moved the number.
