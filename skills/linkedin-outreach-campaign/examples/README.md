# LinkedIn outreach campaign — worked example

`before.txt` is the pitch-in-the-first-message connection request: generic opener, jargon, a demo ask and a 30-minute ask, no reason for the connection. `connect.txt` follows the skill: under 200 characters, one real reason tied to a public interaction, no pitch. `dm.txt` is the first DM after acceptance: continues the exchange, offers something useful, ends on a question, no meeting ask.

Names and the thread are illustrative. No statistics are used. Pinned by `tests/outreach-examples.test.mjs`.
