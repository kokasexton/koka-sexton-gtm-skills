# Re-engagement

A lead goes cold for a reason: timing, priority, the wrong person, or a better option. You can only re-open it when **one of those reasons has changed**, and you have to say what changed.

## The three revival plays

| Play | Trigger | Opening |
|---|---|---|
| **New signal** | They hired, raised, launched, or posted about the problem | "Saw you just hired a Head of RevOps, which usually reopens the stack question…" |
| **New person** | Your contact left; a new leader arrived (see `skills/job-changes`) | To the new leader: the fresh perspective. To the old contact: congrats, no pitch. |
| **New proof** | You have a result, report, or teardown that answers their old objection | "Last time the blocker was X. Here's how a team like yours handled it." |

## Timing

- Closed-lost: revisit when a signal fires, not on a calendar alone. A quarterly check for signals is a sensible default.
- "Not now": ask **when**, and come back then with something new.
- Ghosted mid-deal: one direct, kind note ("Should I close this out?"), then stop.

## Let go when

They opted out, said no twice, or the account no longer fits the ICP. Remove them from sequences. Chasing burns your domain and your name.
