# Buying Signals

A signal is a behaviour that changes the odds of a purchase soon. Prefer behaviour over firmographics.

## Ranked signals

1. **Comment on your content** — highest intent per unit effort. Commenters skew ~26% more senior than likers and 78% write substantive replies.
2. **Repeat engagement** — someone who shows up across multiple posts is a regular, not a passer-by. Warmest cohort in B2B.
3. **Account clustering** — multiple people from one company engaging = account-level intent, visible publicly. Real-time ABM.
4. **Role + timing** — a new VP/CRO/Head of Sales within ~90 days is building a stack and buying.
5. **Funding / headcount moves** — capital or a hiring surge in the function you serve.
6. **Tech-stack change** — adopting or dropping a tool in your category.
7. **Direct intent** — profile views, follows, DMs, pricing-page visits.

## Scoring

Combine **depth** (comment > like > view) × **recency** × **ICP fit** into tiers P0–P3. Sequence only P0/P1. The `skills/warm-list` engine implements this scoring on a real engagement export. A cold-but-perfect-fit account is not a signal — it's a name without a trigger.

## Anti-signals

Job-title labels ("social selling"), follower counts, impressions, and vague "engagement" with no depth. None predict budget.
