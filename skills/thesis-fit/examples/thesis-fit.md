# Thesis fit: Ferngate Ventures (fictional)

Version 1 · 2026-10-31 · Illustrative. Fictional fund, no real people.

## Thesis, in the investor's words

Pre-seed and seed. Payments infrastructure, developer tools and vertical SaaS. US first, Canada and UK second. First cheque into founders who have built at scale before, ideally before they announce.

## Archetypes to surface

1. **Stealth founder:** a senior operator who has just changed their headline to "building".
2. **Repeat operator:** a founder or senior operator on a second build.
3. **Breakout angel:** an operator investing in the thesis space who may co-invest or refer.

## Moves that precede a round (timing, not fit)

- Headline changes to "building" or "stealth".
- A senior role left in the last 90 days.
- Engineering roles opening at their company.

## Hard disqualifiers (any one means excluded)

- Outside the stage, sector or geography above.
- Other funds and their staff, unless the aim is co-investment (then score as a breakout angel).
- Recruiters, students, job seekers.

## Rubric (default, 25 points each, 100 total)

| Dimension | Full points when |
|---|---|
| Stage and sector match | The venture or last company sits squarely in the thesis |
| Founder or operator evidence | Prior founding or a senior operating role at a recognisable company |
| Stated intent | They have said they are building, raising or leaving, in their own words |
| Recency of the move | Within 30 days |

Grades: 70 and up is A, 40 to 69 is B, under 40 is out (the same line as the fit gate in `signal-model`).
