# Skills guide: the LinkedIn warming path, step by step

New to the library? This page is the shortest route from "I have a LinkedIn profile" to "I'm having conversations with the right buyers." Each step names the skill, what it does and a phrase that triggers it. Say the phrase to your agent, or name the skill outright ("Use linkedin-icp-definer").

Nothing here posts, connects, messages or edits for you. Every skill drafts, and you act.

## Quick start

1. **Define who you're targeting.** Everything after this scores against it.
2. **Fix the profile** so attention converts.
3. **See who is already engaging** with your posts.
4. **Rank them** and decide who to warm first.
5. **Research, then engage** before you pitch anything.
6. **Open conversations** with a real reason.
7. **Keep the account safe** the whole way through.

Want steps 2 and the content plan done in one pass? Say "Run my LinkedIn launch" and follow the [launch kit guide](linkedin-launch.md).

## Rules of the road

- Warm first, pitch later. Signals come before messages.
- Every number in a deliverable gets a source. Reach and cadence figures live in [`linkedin-algorithm`](../skills/linkedin-algorithm/SKILL.md) and are cited from there.
- Every send, post and edit needs your approval.
- Not sure where to start? Say: *"Use the koka skill. Where should we start?"*
