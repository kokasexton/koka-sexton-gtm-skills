# Job Changes

New leaders arrive with a mandate, a budget review, and a short window to make their mark. Champions who move take their trust with them.

## Three plays

| Change | Why it matters | Play |
|---|---|---|
| **New leader in your function** at an ICP account | They audit the stack in the first months | Give them something useful for their first-90-day plan |
| **Past champion or happy user moved** to a new company | Trust travels | Congratulate, then offer to help them set up what worked last time |
| **Your contact left** an account | Open deal or account at risk | Find the successor (`skills/find-contacts`); a warm note to the leaver too |

## Timing (default)

- **Week 1–2:** they're onboarding. A short congratulations, or nothing.
- **Roughly weeks 2–12:** they're assessing and planning. This is the window for useful outreach.
- **After ~90 days:** decisions are made. It's still a signal, but a weaker one.

## Where to see it

LinkedIn job-change notifications and Sales Navigator alerts on saved leads, "new hire" announcements on company pages, press releases, and your CRM (bounced emails often mean someone left).

## Message

> Congrats on the move to Northwind, Dana. When you were at Bluepeak, the comment-led prospecting you ran was the best I'd seen. If you're planning something similar here, I'm happy to share what the first 60 days looked like for a team your size.

Make it about **them**, with **one** useful offer, and **no** calendar link.
