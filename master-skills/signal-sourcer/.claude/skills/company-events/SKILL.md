# Company Events

Big events rebuild processes. Rebuilt processes need new tools, new partners, and new playbooks. But not every event opens a window.

## Read the event

| Event | Usually means | Window? |
|---|---|---|
| **Acquisition** (they acquired) | Integrating teams, systems, and messaging | Yes, after the dust settles |
| **Acquired** (they were bought) | Decisions move to the parent; freeze likely | Often no; watch for the new owner |
| **New region or office** | Building a local GTM team from scratch | Yes |
| **New product or segment** | New audience, new messaging, new content | Yes |
| **IPO preparation** | Process rigour, reporting, compliance | Yes, for those functions |
| **Restructure or layoffs** | Cost focus, frozen budgets | No. Be human or be silent. |
| **New CEO/CRO/CMO** | Strategy review | Yes (see `skills/job-changes`) |

## Play

Write to the leader of the function the event rebuilds, about the work the event creates:

> Opening Berlin means building EMEA pipeline from zero. Here's how a team your size sequenced the first 90 days.

## Where to see it

Press releases, company newsroom and blog, executive posts on LinkedIn, local business news, and regulatory filings for public companies.
