# LinkedIn Outreach Campaign

## 1. Pick the list from signals

Start with **P0/P1 from `skills/warm-list`** (people who already engaged), then **ACT NOW / WARM UP accounts from `skills/multi-signal`**. A cold list is last.

## 2. Engage first (days 1–5)

For people who haven't engaged with you: leave a genuine comment on 1–2 of their recent posts, adding a point, not "Great post!". Now there's a reason they'll recognise your name.

## 3. Connection request with a reason

Under ~200 characters, one reason, no pitch.

> Enjoyed your point on SDR ramp in the thread. I'm working on the same problem from the content side. Would be good to be connected.

Skipping the note is fine if you've already interacted publicly. Never use a fake reason.

## 4. First DM: value, no pitch

Send it after they accept. Continue the exchange and offer something useful.

> Thanks for connecting. You mentioned ramp time is the bottleneck. Here's the 1-page shadowing plan from a team your size. No pitch; useful or not?

## 5. One follow-up, then stop

If they reply: have the conversation and earn the ask. If they're quiet after about a week, send one short note adding something new. Then go back to engaging with their content.

## Metrics (track your own baseline)

Acceptance rate → reply rate → positive conversations → meetings. If acceptance is low, fix the targeting or the reason. If replies are low, fix the first DM. Track these in `skills/outbound-metrics`.
