# LinkedIn Safety

Your LinkedIn account is an asset that compounds: your network, your authority, your history. Don't risk it for a week of volume.

## What gets accounts restricted

- Lots of connection requests that **go unaccepted**, or that people mark "I don't know this person"
- Large invitation backlogs (many pending invites)
- Repetitive, identical messages sent quickly
- **Third-party automation** that clicks, scrapes, or sends as you. It's against LinkedIn's User Agreement.
- Sudden spikes in activity on a new or quiet account

## Warm-up ramp (conservative defaults)

| Week | Connection requests / day | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ~5–10 | Mostly people who engaged with you |
| 2 | ~10–15 | Add signal-based targets |
| 3+ | ~15–20, staying well within LinkedIn's weekly invitation limit | Only if acceptance stays high |

Spread the activity through the day like a person would. Withdraw invites that have been pending for weeks.

## Keep acceptance high

Acceptance is the health metric. If it drops, **slow down and fix the targeting and the reason**, rather than pushing volume. Requests to people who engaged with you are accepted far more often than cold ones.

## If restricted

Stop all outreach, complete any verification LinkedIn asks for, remove any automation tools, and come back slowly with warm targets only.
