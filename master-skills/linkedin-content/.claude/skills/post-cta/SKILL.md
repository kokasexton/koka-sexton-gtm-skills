# Post CTA

The post earns attention; the CTA decides what that attention turns into.

## Ask for a comment worth reading

Formulaic CTAs ("Agree?", "Comment YES for the template", "Like if you agree") are **penalised**, and they attract the wrong people. Ask a question that:
- Only someone who's **done the work** can answer.
- Has a **specific** answer: a number, a tool, a story.
- Relates to the post's one lesson.

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| "Thoughts?" | "What's the one signal that's predicted a deal for you this year?" |
| "Agree?" | "If you cut posting to 3×/week, what did you do with the saved time?" |
| "Comment YES for the guide" | "What's the hardest part of working your comment section? I'll answer the top three in a follow-up post." |

## Offering a resource without bait

Mention it plainly and put the link in the first comment. Let people ask; then reply with it. The conversation is the point.

## Make the profile convert

Readers who like a post click your name. The profile has to answer three questions in five seconds: who you help, with what, and what to do next (see `skills/linkedin-profile`).
