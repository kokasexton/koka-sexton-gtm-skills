# Cold Email

The signal is the personalisation. If you have no signal, you have no email — you have a template.

## Structure

1. **One specific observation** — something real about them (a post, a hire, a launch, an engagement). This is the first sentence. No "I hope this finds you well."
2. **One idea** — a relevant insight or data point the recipient would value, connected to the observation.
3. **One ask** — small and specific ("worth a look?" / "open to a quick chat?").
4. **Sign off like a person.**

## Rules

- Short. Under ~120 words for a first touch.
- No fake familiarity — if you didn't read it, don't imply you did.
- No feature dump, no calendar link in touch one.
- Give value before the ask.
- **Never auto-send.** Human gate on everything outbound.

## Sequence

2–3 touches, spaced, each adding something new (a data point, a teardown, a relevant report). Stop on reply. Never send the same email twice with a different name swapped in.

## Signals to anchor on

Comment on their content · a new senior hire · funding · a launch · a tech change · a shared thread. See `skills/buying-signals`.
