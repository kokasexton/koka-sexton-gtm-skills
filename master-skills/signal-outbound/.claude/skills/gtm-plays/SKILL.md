# GTM Plays

A play is a motion with an input signal, a sequence, and a success metric. Run one change at a time so you know what worked.

## The plays

**1. Warm-signal DM (LinkedIn)**
Signal: engaged with your content (comment > like).
Sequence: reference the exact exchange → give one useful idea → small ask.
Metric: reply rate → meetings booked.

**2. Account-cluster ABM**
Signal: multiple people at one company engaging.
Sequence: identify 2–3 stakeholders → personalised touch each → one account thread.
Metric: multi-threaded conversations per account.

**3. Signal-led cold email**
Signal: a trigger (new exec, funding, launch, tech change).
Sequence: observation → idea → ask; 2–3 spaced touches.
Metric: positive-reply rate.

**4. Regular-engager nurture**
Signal: the repeating engagers across many of your posts.
Sequence: name the pattern, invite the conversation.
Metric: warm-reply rate.

**5. Content → inbound**
Signal: a topic your ICP engages with most.
Sequence: publish 3–5×/week on that topic → reply to every comment → route commenters to a play.
Metric: ICP-quality comments → pipeline.

## Rules

- One change at a time; measure before the next.
- Only P0/P1 signals get a sequence (`skills/buying-signals`).
- Give value before the ask, every time.
- Human gate on anything outbound.
