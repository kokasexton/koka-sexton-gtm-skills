# Ads Copy

The best ad copy is plagiarised from your customers, word for word.

## Build a voice-of-customer bank first

Pull exact phrases from:
- **Sales and CS call recordings**: how buyers describe the problem before they knew your category.
- **Reviews** of you and competitors: the complaints are the gold.
- **Comments** on your and your competitors' posts.
- **Lost-deal notes**: the objection, in their words.

Tag each phrase: *pain*, *trigger*, *desired outcome*, *objection*. Write from the bank, not from your positioning doc.

## The shape

1. **Line one (the hook).** The pain or the surprising number, in their words. It has to work before "…see more".
2. **The turn.** One sentence on why it happens, or what changes it.
3. **Proof.** One sourced number or one named pattern.
4. **One offer.** Make it specific and low-friction: "Get the 1-page teardown", not "Learn more".

## Rules

- One idea per ad. Two ideas means two ads, which means a test.
- Headline and intro text say the same thing differently. Don't introduce a new idea in the headline.
- No slop, no superlatives, no "revolutionary". Voice rules apply to paid.
- Make 2–4 variants that each change **one** element (hook, proof, or offer) so the result means something.
