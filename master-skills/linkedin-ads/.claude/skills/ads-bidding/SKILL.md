# Ads Bidding

Bids are the last lever, not the first. A cheap click from the wrong person is expensive.

## Choose the bid strategy by goal

| Situation | Strategy | Why |
|---|---|---|
| New campaign, no history | **Automated (maximum delivery)** | Lets the platform find delivery; gives you a cost baseline |
| You know your acceptable cost per result | **Cost cap / target cost** | Keeps cost predictable once you have a baseline |
| Small ABM audience, must reach everyone | **Manual bid** | You control competitiveness for a narrow, high-value audience |

## Budget allocation

- **By layer.** Most early spend goes to layer 1 (it fills the retargeting pools), shifting toward layers 2–3 as pools grow. Retargeting pools are small, so their budgets are small.
- **By tier.** Tier-1 accounts get disproportionate spend per account.
- **Don't spread thin.** Too many campaigns on too little budget means none of them get enough delivery to learn from. Fewer, better, the same rule as organic.

## When costs spike

1. Check the audience isn't too small or saturated (frequency climbing).
2. Check creative fatigue (engagement falling).
3. Check competition (seasonality, end of quarter).
4. Only then change the bid, in one step, and give it time before judging.
