# Ads → Outbound Sync

An ad that earned engagement from a target account is a signal. It's only worth something if a person acts on it while it's warm.

## What counts as a signal

| Strong | Medium | Not a signal |
|---|---|---|
| Lead-form open or submit, document fully viewed, comment on a Thought Leader Ad, pricing-page visit after an ad | Several people at one account engaging, repeat video completion | A single impression or a lone click |

## The handoff

1. **Weekly export** of engaged companies (company engagement report) and, where available, engaged people (form opens, comments on sponsored posts, Thought Leader Ad engagers).
2. **Score** with the other signals: hiring, funding, website visits, organic engagement (see `skills/multi-signal`). Ads alone rarely justify outreach; ads plus one more signal often do.
3. **Route with context.** The SDR sees which ad they engaged with and what it taught, so the outreach continues the same story.
4. **Coordinate the message.** If the ad was the SDR-team teardown, the email offers the full teardown, not a generic demo.

## The message

> Your team spent time with our breakdown of comment-led prospecting this week. Happy to send the full version, including how a team your size staffs it. Useful?

Talk about the topic and the help, not about tracking them. Never say "I saw you clicked our ad."
