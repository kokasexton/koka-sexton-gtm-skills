# What is engagement intelligence?

Engagement intelligence is a method for reading who actually engages with a company's or executive's content — classifying every engager off their own headline by seniority, function, and ICP fit — so that marketing and sales act on real buyer behaviour instead of impressions.

_By Koka Sexton · Updated November 2026_

## How does engagement intelligence work?

It runs in five steps:

1. Capture public posts and engagers for a profile or company page.
2. Classify each engager by seniority and function from their own headline.
3. Score each person against the ICP.
4. Read the room: peer-heavy or buyer-heavy?
5. Surface the regulars who engage again and again.

## Why do commenters matter more than likers?

Commenters are 44.2% Director+ versus 35.1% of likers — 26% more senior — according to the [LinkedIn Algorithm Report 2026](https://kokasexton.com/insights), where we analyzed 2,279 posts from 246 creators.

## Engagement intelligence vs. social listening

| | Engagement intelligence | Social listening |
|---|---|---|
| Unit | A person who engaged | A mention of a keyword |
| Output | A tiered warm list | A sentiment trend |
| Used for | Pipeline, ABM | Brand monitoring |

## FAQ

### Is engagement intelligence the same as intent data?

No. Intent data infers interest from anonymous research behaviour; engagement intelligence uses named, public engagement with your own content.

### Which tools does it need?

An export of post engagers and a classification rubric. The `warm-list` skill in this repo does the scoring.
