# SDR Tools

A great SDR is a researcher who writes well and follows up kindly. The tools should make research faster and writing better, not replace either one with volume.

## The kit

| Need | Sub-skill |
|---|---|
| Agent prompts: research an account, draft from a signal, handle a reply | `skills/sdr-prompts` |
| The team's writing rules in one page | `skills/sdr-writing-rules` |
| Cold call opener, voicemail, and no-show scripts | `skills/cold-call-scripts` |
| Metrics, baselines, and the activity a target needs | `skills/outbound-metrics` |

## A day, the Koka way

1. **Warm first** (30 min): reply to comments, check P0/P1 in `skills/warm-list`.
2. **Signals** (30 min): ACT NOW accounts from `skills/multi-signal`.
3. **Research and write** (2 hours): 5 minutes of research per person (`skills/personalization-sources`), one message per signal.
4. **Calls** (1 hour): ACT NOW accounts with phone numbers you're allowed to call.
5. **Engage** (15 min): genuine comments on ICP posts.
6. **Log** what replied and why.
