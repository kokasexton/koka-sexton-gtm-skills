# Outreach Motions

Every conversation starts from one of four places. The warmer the start, the less you have to explain who you are.

| Motion | Starts with | Needs | Best for | Skill |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Inbound** | They came to you (form, demo request, DM) | Fast, helpful response; routing | Highest intent; don't make them wait | `skills/sdr-prompts` |
| **Warm** | They engaged with you (commented, attended, repeat engager, visited) | Engagement data plus a reason to talk | The most underworked motion in B2B | `skills/warm-list` |
| **Triggered** | Something changed for them (hire, funding, tech change) | Signal monitoring, timing | Reaching in-market accounts first | `master-skills/signal-sourcer` |
| **Cold** | Fit only, no signal | A great list and a sharp idea | Filling gaps in tier-1 coverage | `master-skills/cold-email` |

## The mix

- **Fill warm first.** Most teams leave their commenters, regulars, and visitors unworked while they buy cold lists.
- **Triggered second.** Signals tell you who's in-market.
- **Cold last**, aimed at tier-1 accounts with no signal yet, and designed to **create** warmth (a useful asset, an invitation to engage) rather than to book a meeting immediately.

## Move people up

Cold → **warm**: they engage with content, comment, or attend. Treat that as the conversion.
Warm → **conversation**: a reply on a real exchange (see `skills/post-engagement`).
Triggered + warm → **priority**: work it this week (see `skills/multi-signal`).
