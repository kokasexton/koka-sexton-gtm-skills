# GTM Strategy

Strategy is choosing what **not** to do. Most teams run every motion at half strength. The fix is fewer motions, run as a system, measured honestly.

## The order of decisions

1. **Principles:** how this team decides → `skills/gtm-principles`
2. **Who:** the ICP → `skills/define-icp`
3. **Which motion:** inbound, warm, triggered, or cold, and in what mix → `skills/outreach-motions`
4. **Why now:** the triggers you'll watch → `skills/sales-triggers`
5. **Which plays:** signal → sequence → metric → `skills/gtm-plays`
6. **How you'll know:** the metric for each play → `skills/outbound-metrics`

## The diagnostic

When pipeline is weak, ask in order:
- Is the **ICP** real (from won deals) or aspirational?
- Is there a **signal** behind every outreach, or is it list-and-blast?
- Does **content** bring the right people into the room (Director+, ICP-fit), or just peers?
- Is anyone **working the warm audience** (commenters, regulars, visitors)?
- Is **one change at a time** being measured?

The answer is usually the earliest "no".

## The Koka system in one line

**Content** earns attention → **engagement** shows who's in the room → **signals** say why now → **outreach** is written to the signal → **measurement** feeds back into content.
