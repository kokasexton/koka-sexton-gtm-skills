# Personalization Hooks

## Strong hooks

Built from **buckets 1–3** in `skills/personalization-sources`: their words, their engagement, a change. They're specific to the person and connected to your reason.

| Observation | Strong hook |
|---|---|
| Their post on SDR ramp time | "Your post on cutting SDR ramp from 90 to 45 days had the most practical comment thread I've read this month: the bit about shadowing first calls especially." |
| Commented on your post | "You asked in the thread how to separate buyers from peers. Here's the rubric." |
| New VP, 3 weeks in | "Three weeks into the VP Marketing seat at Northwind, the stack review is probably on the list." |

Use strong hooks for **tier-1 accounts and P0/P1 people**.

## Light hooks

Built from **segment or company context**. They're honest about not being deeply personal, and still relevant.

| Context | Light hook |
|---|---|
| Industry pattern | "Most RevOps teams at Series B SaaS companies hit the same wall around 20 reps…" |
| Company news | "With the Berlin office opening, EMEA pipeline is probably the next question." |

Use light hooks for **tier-2/3 at scale**. Never pretend a light hook is a strong one ("I was just reading about your company…").

## The bridge

Every hook needs a second sentence that says **why it matters to what you're offering**. If you can't write the bridge, pick a different observation.
